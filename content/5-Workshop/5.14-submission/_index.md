Use this checklist before submitting the Workshop section.

- [ ] Overview, prerequisites, architecture and data flow, lab steps, testing, clean-up and demo each have their own page.
- [ ] The demo video opens and shows the customer pages and the admin screen.
- [ ] Every internal link and image loads in both languages (links are written in lower case).
- [ ] No password, access key, session secret or personal data appears in a page or screenshot.
- [ ] The commands match the files in the group repository.
- [ ] The limits and what was not tested (load test, restore drill, email queue) are stated honestly.
- [ ] The evidence links to the group site open.
- [ ] The cost figures, dates and names are the same on every page.

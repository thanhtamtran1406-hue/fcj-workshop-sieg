**1. Impressions of the FCAJ internship program**
The program is well organized and gave me a practical start with AWS. I reviewed AWS fundamentals, IAM, cost management, CloudTrail and GuardDuty, and I worked with my team to deploy a serverless application. The events, including AWS First Day Cloud, the watch party and the fireside chat with Dr. Werner Vogels, widened my view of cloud and AI.

**2. Level of satisfaction**
Very satisfied (Good).

**3. Points to improve**
Announcing the event schedule and the report requirements earlier would help students plan their time.

**4. Would you recommend the program to friends? Why?**
Yes. The program combines self-study, real events and a team project, so students gain hands-on experience and a portfolio they can show.
